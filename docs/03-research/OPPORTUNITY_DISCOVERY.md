# Opportunity Discovery

## Market Opportunity vs Product Concept

A Market Opportunity describes **what is underserved and why it matters**. A Product Concept describes **what we could build to address it**.

Example:

- Opportunity: Arabic-speaking nurses relocating to Germany struggle to practice job-specific spoken German with culturally relevant scenarios.
- Concept A: mobile AI role-play coach for hospital conversations.
- Concept B: spaced-repetition phrase trainer with pronunciation feedback.

The system must not collapse these into one entity.

## Opportunity Generation Inputs

- signal clusters;
- recurring user-voice clusters;
- competitor weaknesses;
- country/language asymmetry;
- technology/platform changes;
- pricing/business-model gaps;
- distribution shifts;
- category momentum.

## Opportunity Required Fields

- concise title;
- problem statement;
- job-to-be-done;
- target audience;
- why-now;
- opportunity type;
- relevant app/game category;
- primary markets;
- key signals;
- key unknowns;
- initial evidence confidence;
- first/last observed.

## Product Concept Required Fields

- title;
- one-sentence value proposition;
- target user;
- source opportunity;
- wedge;
- differentiation hypothesis;
- MVP thesis;
- monetization candidates;
- distribution candidates;
- primary risks;
- technical dependencies;
- platform scope;
- AI dependency classification: none/supporting/core;
- why a solo AI-assisted Flutter developer could/could not execute it.

## Opportunity Types

Use only canonical types:

- `ORIGINAL_INNOVATION`
- `COMPETITOR_IMPROVEMENT`
- `NICHE_SPECIALIZATION`
- `LOCALIZATION_ARBITRAGE`
- `BUSINESS_MODEL_INNOVATION`
- `PLATFORM_EXPANSION`
- `FEATURE_UNBUNDLING`
- `FEATURE_BUNDLING`
- `TREND_EXPLOITATION`
- `WORKFLOW_REPLACEMENT`

## Candidate Selection for Deep Research

Use a cheap preliminary rank based on:

- signal strength/recency;
- evidence diversity;
- novelty vs existing opportunity graph;
- local/global relevance;
- preliminary solo-builder fit;
- apparent competitor gap;
- configured category preferences.

This rank is not the final Opportunity Score.

## Clone Rule

A concept that is semantically “same app, nicer UI” fails wedge validation. Valid wedges include evidence-backed:

- specialized audience;
- underserved market/language;
- materially different workflow;
- simpler/cheaper business model;
- missing cross-platform availability;
- strong recurring competitor complaint solved at product level;
- distribution advantage;
- technical capability newly possible.
