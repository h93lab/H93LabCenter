# Product Vision

## Vision

Create a personal intelligence system that continuously converts changing mobile-market evidence into actionable product opportunities and then into implementation-ready Flutter project specifications.

The platform should become the owner's answer to: **“What should I build next, and what evidence justifies that decision?”**

## Primary Outcome

Reduce wasted development time by filtering weak opportunities before implementation and by eliminating ambiguity after a GO decision.

## User Job-to-be-Done

> When I am deciding what mobile app or game to build next, I want a system that continuously researches the market, exposes demand and competitive gaps with evidence, compares opportunities using consistent rules, and generates a precise product blueprint so I can start implementation with an AI coding agent without inventing requirements during coding.

## Product Pillars

### 1. Autonomous Discovery
The user should not need to start with an idea. Daily research discovers signals and candidate opportunities automatically.

### 2. Evidence Before Recommendation
Recommendations must be explainable, sourced, time-aware, and confidence-rated.

### 3. Mobile-Specific Intelligence
The system understands app-store dynamics, mobile monetization, retention, distribution, platform constraints, localization, and solo-developer feasibility.

### 4. Global + Local Opportunity
Each opportunity can have different attractiveness by market. A local breakout is valid even if its global score is moderate.

### 5. Decision Clarity
The interface starts with analysis and numbers. The optional Executive Brief answers “build, validate, watch, pass, or kill?” in concise terms.

### 6. Research-to-Build Traceability
Evidence should survive the transition to the Blueprint. A complaint or market gap can be traced into a product decision, feature, screen, rule, task, and test.

### 7. AI Without AI Theater
Use AI where language/reasoning provides leverage, but keep deterministic systems for scoring, state, budgets, permissions, and validation.

## Success Measures

V1 is successful when:

- daily research runs complete reliably without manual intervention;
- discovered opportunities are deduplicated and historically tracked;
- every promoted idea has evidence-backed reasoning and visible confidence;
- the owner can compare opportunities by global and country-level scores;
- the owner can press GO and receive a coherent Blueprint without manually assembling documents;
- AI change requests update all affected Blueprint artifacts consistently;
- a Blueprint can pass quality gates and be exported for a Flutter-focused coding agent;
- the platform's AI/research spending stays within configured budgets.

## Anti-Goals

- do not become a generic startup idea generator;
- do not become a general web-search chat assistant;
- do not attempt to recreate Sensor Tower's entire proprietary dataset;
- do not become a team project manager;
- do not become a code-generation IDE;
- do not treat attractive UI as a substitute for trustworthy evidence.
